## When to use it

Use Voice memo for quick spoken notes: one take, started and stopped with a
press. The first press wakes the device; later presses start and stop
listening. Words fill a live transcript beside it, which saves as a `.md`
file. It uses the browser's speech recognition, or shows text from another
service through `transcript` and `interim`. This example types out a script
in place of the microphone.

## Accessibility

- Asleep, the device is a button named Wake; Enter or Space wakes it and
  announces Ready. Awake, it's a toggle button named Transcribe, pressed while
  listening. Enter or Space starts and stops it; focus stays on it.
- State doesn't rely on the light's color: the button's name and pressed state
  convey asleep, ready or listening.
- Start and stop are announced once in a status region, with the word count on
  stop. Words aren't read out as they arrive, which would talk over the
  speaker.
- The transcript is a region the device references with aria-controls. Its
  text is a focusable log, scrollable and readable by keyboard.
- Once stopped, Tab moves from the device to copy, download and close.
- Failures, such as a blocked microphone or a browser without recognition,
  show in the transcript and are announced.
- Under reduced motion, the device doesn't hop, shake, lift or press in, the
  light holds still, and the transcript fades in without growing.
