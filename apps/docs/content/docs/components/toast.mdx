## When to use it

Use Toast to confirm something that just happened, such as a save, or to
report a background event, such as a failed upload. Mount `Toaster` once
near the app root, then call `toast.add({ title, description })` from
anywhere, even outside React. It returns an id for `toast.close` and
`toast.update`. Toasts stack in the bottom corner and fan out on hover or
focus. Anything people must read or act on belongs on the page or in a
dialog.

## Accessibility

- Each toast is announced through a live region as it appears. A
  high-priority toast interrupts; the rest wait for a pause.
- F6 moves focus to the Notifications region, and Tab reaches each toast.
  Escape closes the focused toast.
- A toast doesn't time out while the pointer is over the stack or focus is
  inside it.
- The Dismiss button is hidden from screen readers until the stack expands,
  so it isn't announced with every message.
- Type icons are decorative. Put the meaning in the title: “Upload failed”,
  not just a red icon.
