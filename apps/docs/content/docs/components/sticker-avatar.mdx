## When to use it

Use Sticker avatar where people and personality are the point. A transparent
image gets a white paper edge that follows its outline; a photo with a
background becomes a round sticker, and with no image the initials are cut
out. Each sticker tilts by a stable angle from the name and lifts on hover.
The edge is drawn once when the image loads, so fifty stickers cost no more
than fifty avatars.

Pass an image with its background already removed. Cutting a subject out in
the browser takes a 40 to 170 MB model, so do it once on upload, on a server,
or let people bring their own. Cross-origin images need CORS headers for the
edge to be cut; without them the sticker falls back to round.

<Install

Installs Status dot and adds `--sticker-edge`, `--sticker-ink` and
`--sticker-shadow` to your theme.

## With a status

A [Status dot](/docs/components/status-dot) child. The sticker sets it on the
corner, turned against the tilt, and adds its label to the sticker's name.
Present, away and offline are a dot, a crescent and a ring, so each reads
without color.

## In a direct message list

At 32px the edge and tilt suit a dense list. Each row carries
`group/sticker`, so hovering anywhere on it lifts the sticker.

## Accessibility

- The root is `role="img"`, named by `name` and a StatusDot's label through
  `aria-labelledby`: “Ana Ruiz, Away”. Pass the dot a `label` to translate the
  status.
- Inside a row or button that already says the name, set `aria-hidden` on the
  sticker so the name isn't read twice.
- Status never relies on color alone. Each state has its own shape, and its
  label is in the accessible name.
- The hit area and layout are the square box, not the cut, so an odd shape is
  no harder to click.
- With reduced motion the sticker keeps its tilt and shadow but doesn't lift.
