## When to use it

Use `DatePicker` for one day, such as a due date: it closes as soon as a day
is chosen. Use `DateRangePicker` for a span, such as a report period or a
stay: it keeps a draft with presets and applies both ends together. Below the
`sm` breakpoint both open as a drawer from the bottom.

## Range

Two picks, a live preview between them, the range and its length in the
footer, then Apply. Cancel and Escape leave the value as it was. `onChange`
fires on Apply, never on a half-picked range.

## Presets and bounds

Presets are functions of today, so "Last 7 days" stays right tomorrow. A
preset picks and closes, like a day. Days outside `minDate`, `maxDate` or
`isDateDisabled` are struck through, and a preset that lands on one is
disabled.

## Accessibility

- The trigger is a button named by the label and the value, such as Date
  range: Oct 5 – 11, 2026. Inside a Field, the FieldLabel and the value name
  it instead, and clicking the label focuses it. The panel is a dialog named
  by the label, which the drawer also shows as its title.
- Opening moves focus to the picked day, or today, so the arrows work at
  once. Escape closes and returns focus to the trigger.
- The calendar's grid keyboard model applies inside; see Calendar.
- Presets are toggle buttons: the one matching the draft is `aria-pressed`.
- Apply stays disabled until both ends are picked, and the footer says what's
  missing.
- Day cells in the drawer are 44 pixels, above the minimum touch target.
