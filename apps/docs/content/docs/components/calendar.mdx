## When to use it

Use Calendar alone when the dates are the content, such as availability on a
booking page. In a form, use Date picker, which opens a Calendar from a field.
`type="scroll"` stacks a year of months for a phone drawer, and
`type="month"` fills a page with a month of events.

## Installation

<Install name="calendar" exports={["Calendar"]} />

Installs Button and `lib/dates.ts`, its date helpers. `useToday` is exported
too.

## Range

`mode="range"` picks a start and an end, drawn as one bar per week, with a
preview under the pointer before the second pick. Two months sit side by side
and stack when there isn't room.

## Disabled days

Days before `minDate`, after `maxDate` or rejected by `isDateDisabled` are
struck through and can't be picked. They still take keyboard focus.

  </div>
</Preview>

## Month view

`type="month"` is a month of events that fills its parent, so the parent needs
a height. Each cell shows its day, the first two events and a count of the
rest; picking a day lists all its events. Below 768px wide the cells shrink to
the day and up to three dots.

## Accessibility

- Each month is a grid labeled by its title. Column headers carry the full
  weekday name, and each day button the full date, such as Tuesday, October
  20, 2026.
- The grid is one tab stop. Arrows move by a day or a week, Home and End to
  the ends of the week, Page Up and Page Down by a month, and with Shift by a
  year. Moving past the page turns it.
- Enter or Space picks the focused day. The picked days' cells are
  `aria-selected`, and today is `aria-current`.
- Unpickable days are `aria-disabled` rather than disabled, so they stay in
  the keyboard path and are announced as unavailable.
- In range mode a polite live region says what happened: the start and that
  an end is needed, then the range and its length.
- In the month type each cell holds one day button, so the grid keeps the
  same keyboard model. Cards and dots are hidden from assistive technology.
  The button is described by a list of the day's events instead.
- The month type's events become buttons in the selected day's list, outside
  the grid, so nothing inside a cell takes focus. Tab moves from the grid to
  that list.
- The page slide fades in and is off under reduced motion.
