## When to use it

Use Chapter scrubber to move through sequential content: a talk, a long read,
a case study, a recording. Marks swell around the pointer or keyboard focus,
Dock-style, and the tallest mark (the crest) previews its chapter as a card,
a label or nothing. It runs vertical or horizontal, with ticks or dots, in
three densities. Under reduced motion the springs switch off; the wave keeps
its shape but appears and follows instantly.

## Installation

<Install

## Horizontal

For a player or slide deck. Ticks rise from the baseline and the preview
opens above.

## Guidelines

<UsageGuidelines
  guidelines={[
    "Use for sequential content: a talk, a long read, a case study, a recording.",
    "Keep six to thirty chapters. Fewer reads as a list; more stops the wave reaching neighbors.",
    <>
      Use <code>card</code> when chapters have descriptions, <code>label</code>{" "}
      when the title suffices.
    </>,
    <>
      Leave <code>side</code> unset in most layouts. It prefers right or top and

      opens on the roomier side and narrows to fit. It closes once the rail
      scrolls out of view.
    </>,
    <>
      Set <code>currentIndex</code> from scroll or playback position so the
      marker tracks the reader between clicks.
    </>,
  ]}

## Accessibility

- The rail is a listbox; each mark is an option named by its title and
  description. The current chapter is `aria-selected`.
- One mark is in the tab order. Arrow keys, Home and End move along the rail;
  Enter or Space makes a chapter current.
- Focus raises the wave and preview exactly as hover does.
