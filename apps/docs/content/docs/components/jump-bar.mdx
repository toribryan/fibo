## When to use it

Use Jump bar over a scrolling conversation, such as a channel or a support
chat, when the reader isn't looking at the messages that matter. It comes in
three types: unread messages above with "Mark as read", a pill for new
messages below, and a bar while viewing history.

## Installation

<Install name="jump-bar" exports={["JumpBar", "jumpTo", "useAtBottom"]} />

## Types

`new-below` is a pill over the bottom of the conversation, for messages that
arrived while scrolled up. `history` is a bar shown while reading older
messages, to go back to the present.

## Over 99

Counts cap at 99+ on screen. The button's name keeps the visible words and
adds the full number, "99+ new messages (128)", so speech input can still say
what's on screen.

<Preview>
  <div className="relative h-32 w-96 max-w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-border bg-card">
    <JumpBar type="new-below" count={128} />
  </div>
</Preview>

The bars position themselves. Place them in a `relative` wrapper around the
scroll container, not inside it. `jumpTo(target, { container, block })`
scrolls a message into view and focuses it, and `useAtBottom(element)` says
whether the conversation is scrolled to its end.

## Accessibility

- Every bar is plain buttons, reachable by Tab. A capped count keeps its
  visible words in the name and adds the full number (WCAG 2.5.3).
- A jump moves focus to the first unread or newest message; `jumpTo` does
  this. Never leave focus on a button whose bar has disappeared. After "Mark
  as read", move it to the composer.
- `jumpTo` scrolls instantly under reduced motion, and for any jump longer
  than three screens.
- The unread divider carries a "New" label, so the marker isn't color alone.
