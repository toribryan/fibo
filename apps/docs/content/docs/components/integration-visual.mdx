## When to use it

Use Integration visual to show a product and the tools wired into it, at the
top of a card or a marketing section. Pulses run along live routes, inward,
outward, or through the hub from left to right, while idle tools keep their
route and dim. Hovering or focusing the hub opens a preview: any content, an
image or a looping video. Under reduced motion the pulses, halo and springs
stop, the preview fades in, and a preview video holds its first frame.

Items fill the layout's slots in order; any past its limit are dropped. Each
`title` names its tile for screen readers.

<Install

Installs `motion`, for the pulses and the halo.

## Media preview

`preview={{ src, alt }}` shows an image, an animated image, or a muted,
looping video. Media is 16:9 unless `aspectRatio` is set. Hover or focus the
snail.

## Guidelines

<UsageGuidelines
  guidelines={[
    <>
      Choose the layout by count: <code>corners</code> for four,{" "}
      <code>orbit</code> for five to eight, <code>sides</code> when data flows
      in one side and out the other.
    </>,
    <>
      Mark tools that aren't connected yet as <code>idle</code> rather than
      leaving them out, so the diagram shows what's possible as well as live.
    </>,
    <>
      Use <code>pulse="inward"</code> for sources feeding the hub,{" "}
      <code>"outward"</code> for the hub sending to them, and{" "}
      <code>"through"</code> for a pipeline that flows from left to right.
    </>,
    <>
      Name the hub with the product, a count, or a mark in <code>center</code>.
      Keep it to a word or a number.
    </>,
    "Put it at the top of a card and let the card's text make the claim.",
  ]}
/>

<Tip>
  Keyboard users can't reach anything inside the preview. Put links in the card
  around the diagram instead.
</Tip>

## Accessibility

- The diagram is a labeled group, and each tool is a list item named by its
  title, with ", idle" added for idle ones. Set `label` to say what the
  diagram is.
- Routes, pulses and the halo are decorative and hidden from assistive tech.
- When there's a preview the hub is a button, named by `centerLabel` (or by
  `center` when that's text, or by `label`). Focus shows the preview as hover
  does, the preview describes the hub, and Escape closes it.
