## When to use it

Use Annotated text for emphasis in display type and editorial copy. The marks
are drawn as SVG in the text's own pixels, so they keep their proportions at
any type size, and their wobble is seeded from the text, so a phrase looks
the same on every render. `animate` draws a mark in on first scroll into
view. Keep annotated phrases short: they never wrap, since a mark is drawn
round one line.

## Sticker chips

`AnnotatedTextChip` puts a sticker at the pill's start, tilted by an amount
derived from the word. An emoji or `<img>` gets a white die-cut edge round its
outline; a line icon is printed in ink on a paper tile.

## Cycling chips

`AnnotatedTextCycle` swaps word and sticker in one pill, which springs to
fit. It pauses under the pointer and, by default, runs through once and
settles on the last item.

## Accessibility

- Marks and stickers are hidden from assistive technology. If a mark carries
  meaning, like a struck-out price, say so in the text too.
- `AnnotatedTextCycle` gives screen readers one fixed word: `label`, or the
  first item. The moving pill is hidden from them.
- By default the cycle runs through once and stops. A looping cycle moves for
  more than five seconds, so pair `loop` with a control that sets `paused`
  (WCAG 2.2.2). It also pauses under the pointer.
- Under prefers-reduced-motion marks appear whole without drawing in, and a
  cycle holds on its first item.
- Check that text over a highlight keeps its contrast in both themes.
