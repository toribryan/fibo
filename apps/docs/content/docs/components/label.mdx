## When to use it

Use Label to name any form control. Clicking it focuses or toggles the
control, and it dims with its control when the control is disabled. Match
`htmlFor` to the control's `id`, or wrap the control in the label.

<Install name="label" exports={["Label"]} />

## Guidelines

<UsageGuidelines
  guidelines={[
    "Write labels as short nouns or questions, in sentence case, with no colon.",
    "Place the label above inputs and textareas, and to the right of checkboxes.",
    "Mark the optional fields, not the required ones, when most fields are required.",
  ]}
/>

## Accessibility

- Connect every label with `htmlFor` or by wrapping the control.
- Dims with a disabled control, so the pair reads as one unit.
