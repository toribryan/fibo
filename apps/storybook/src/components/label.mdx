# Label

A label names a form control. Clicking it focuses or toggles the control, so
it also widens the hit area.

<Playground of={Stories.Default} />

## Import

<Install name="label" exports={["Label"]} />

## Usage

<UsageGuidelines
  guidelines={[
    "Write labels as short nouns or questions, in sentence case, with no colon.",
    "Place the label above inputs and textareas, and to the right of checkboxes.",
    "Mark the optional fields, not the required ones, when most fields are required.",
  ]}
/>

## Accessibility

<UsageGuidelines
  guidelines={[
    <>
      Always connect a label with <code>htmlFor</code> or by wrapping the
      control.
    </>,
    "A label dims with its control when the control is disabled, so the pair reads as one unit.",
  ]}
