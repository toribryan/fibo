# Reactions

Reactions let people respond to a piece of content with an emoji in one tap.
They come in three shapes that share the same choices, pill motion and
particle burst.

<Canvas of={Stories.InMessage} />

## Usage

<UsageGuidelines
  guidelines={[
    "Use inline beside the content it belongs to, such as a message, comment or post.",
    "Use floating for a page-level reaction, such as the end of an article. It pins to a viewport corner.",
    "Use menu when the corner button also carries navigation. The menu slot takes any items.",
    "Offer five or six choices at most. More turns a quick tap into a decision.",
  ]}
/>

<Tip>
  Pass <code>reactions</code> and <code>onReactionsChange</code> to control the
  state from your data layer. Without them the component keeps its own state,
  seeded from <code>defaultReactions</code>.
</Tip>

## Accessibility

<UsageGuidelines
  guidelines={[
    "Each pill is a toggle button with an accessible name from the reaction's label, not the emoji.",
