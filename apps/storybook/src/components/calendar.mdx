# Calendar

A month grid for picking one day or a range. It works on its own on a page,
and it's the grid inside Date picker and Date range picker.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## Features

- `mode="range"` picks a start and an end. The range is drawn as one bar
  per week, and while the second day is chosen it previews under the
  pointer or the keyboard.
- The whole keyboard model of a date grid: arrows by day and week, Home and
  End, Page Up and Page Down by month, Shift for a year.
- Bounds and rules: `minDate`, `maxDate` and `isDateDisabled`, with the
  week starting on Sunday or Monday and names in any locale.
- `type="scroll"` stacks a year either side in one vertical scroll with
  44 pixel days, for a drawer on a phone.
- `type="month"` fills its parent with one month of events and lists the
  selected day's. In a narrow column it switches to compact cells with
  dots.

## Examples

### Range

Two months side by side. The first pick sets the start, and the bar
follows the pointer until the second; picking an earlier day orders the
range. Where there isn't room for two months side by side, they stack;
the days never shrink below their size.

<Canvas of={Stories.Range} />

### Disabled days

Days before `minDate`, after `maxDate` or rejected by `isDateDisabled` are
struck through and can't be picked. They still take focus, so the keyboard
walks through them.

<Canvas of={Stories.DisabledDays} />

### Week starts Monday

`weekStartsOn={1}` puts Monday in the first column. Home and End follow it.

<Canvas of={Stories.WeekStartsMonday} />

### Locale

Month names, weekdays and day numbers come from `Intl` in the `locale`
given.

<Canvas of={Stories.Locale} />

## Month view

`type="month"` is a full-page month of events: the month and its date span
as the title, Previous, Today and Next, and a grid of bordered day cells
that fills its parent. Each cell shows its day and the first two events as
cards, then a count of the rest. Picking a day lists all its events, where
`onEventClick` makes them buttons: beside the grid from 1024 pixels wide,
under it below that. It picks one day, with the same `value`, `onChange`,
`month` and bounds as the other types.

<Canvas of={Stories.Month} />

### On a phone

The layout follows the calendar's own width, not the screen's. Below 768
pixels the cells shrink to the day and up to three dots, and the selected
day's events fill the space under the grid.

<Canvas of={Stories.MonthOnAPhone} />

### Empty

A day with no events says so in the list.

<Canvas of={Stories.MonthEmpty} />

### Many events

A cell shows two cards and counts the rest; the selected day's list has
them all.

<Canvas of={Stories.MonthWithManyEvents} />

### With actions

Children go at the end of the header, for actions such as search and a new
event. The other types ignore children.

<Canvas of={Stories.MonthWithActions} />

```tsx
type CalendarEvent = {
  id: string
  title: string
  start: Date // the day it shows on
  end?: Date // same day
  allDay?: boolean
}
;<div className="flex h-dvh flex-col p-6">
  <Calendar
    type="month"
    events={events}
    value={day}
    onChange={setDay}
    onEventClick={(event) => openEvent(event.id)}
  >
    <Button size="sm" onClick={newEvent}>
      <PlusIcon data-icon="inline-start" />
      New event
    </Button>
  </Calendar>
</div>
```

The month view fills its parent, so give the parent a height. It picks one
day: with `mode="range"` it draws the paged type and warns in the console.
Its English words (Today, +N more, No events, All day) come from `labels`.

## Usage

```tsx
import { Calendar } from "@/components/ui/calendar"

export function Availability() {
  const [range, setRange] = useState<{ from: Date; to?: Date } | null>(null)
  return (
    <Calendar
      mode="range"
      months={2}
      value={range}
      onChange={setRange}
      minDate={new Date()}
    />
  )
}
```

In range mode `onChange` fires after each pick: with only `from` after the
first, with both after the second. Wait for `to` before you act on it.

## Guidelines

<UsageGuidelines
  guidelines={[
    "Use Calendar on its own when the dates are the content, such as availability on a booking page. In a form, use Date picker so the field takes one line until it's needed.",
    "Show two months for a range that often crosses a month end, such as a stay or a report period. One is enough for a single day.",
    <>
      Set bounds with <code>minDate</code> and <code>maxDate</code> rather than

    </>,
    "Start the week where your readers do: Monday in most of Europe, Sunday in the US.",
