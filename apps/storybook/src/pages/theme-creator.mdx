# Theme creator

Make fibo your own without leaving its rules behind. Tint the greys, round the
corners, give primary a hue, move the status colours or change the fonts, and
watch real fibo parts take it on in both modes. Every pair of text and fill
the system relies on is checked against WCAG AA as you go.
