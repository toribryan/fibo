# Motion

Motion in fibo shows where something came from and where it went. It is short,
eases out, and stops under reduced motion.

## Durations

Four durations, from Tailwind's scale. The shorter the distance, the shorter
the duration.

<DurationScale />

## Entering and leaving

Popups such as Menu, Select and Tooltip fade and scale up from 95% toward their
trigger over 100ms, and reverse on exit, with `tw-animate-css`'s `animate-in`
and `animate-out`. Sheets and toasts slide in from their edge.

<EnterExit />

## Springs

Special parts move with `motion`, on springs rather than durations, so an
interrupted move continues from where it is. A stiff, well-damped spring
settles without bouncing. A looser one overshoots slightly, reserved for
physical moments such as Floating nav's glass lens or a reaction landing. Base
parts use CSS only.

## Reduced motion

Every animation in fibo has a `motion-reduce` fallback or reads
`useReducedMotion()`: popups appear in place, indicators jump instead of
sliding, and continuous animation such as a progress ring or typing dots
stops.

<Tip>
  Continuous animation stays off unless it is the point of the part. A progress
  ring is; a decorative shimmer isn't.
</Tip>

## Rules

- Ease out for things arriving, so they slow into place.
- Prefer `transform` and `opacity`. Animating width or height triggers layout
  on every frame, so limit it to small, contained parts, such as a nav label
  growing into view.
- Keep motion under 300ms for anything a person waits on.
