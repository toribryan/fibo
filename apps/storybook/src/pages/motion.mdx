# Motion

Motion in fibo says where something came from and where it went. It is short,
it eases out, and it stops entirely when someone asks for less motion.

## Durations

Four durations, from Tailwind's scale. The shorter the trip, the shorter the
time. Press Play to compare them.

<DurationScale />

## Entering and leaving

Popups fade and scale up from 95% toward what opened them, and leave the same
way in reverse, over 100ms: Menu, Select and Tooltip all do this, with
`tw-animate-css`'s `animate-in` and `animate-out`. Sheets and toasts slide in
from their edge instead.

<EnterExit />

## Springs

Parts on the Special shelf move with `motion`, on springs rather than
durations, so a move that's interrupted carries on from where it is. A stiff,
well-damped spring settles quickly without bouncing; a looser one overshoots
a little, which fibo keeps for moments meant to feel physical, such as
Floating nav's glass lens or a reaction landing. Base parts use CSS only.

## Reduced motion

Every animation in fibo has a `motion-reduce` fallback or reads
`useReducedMotion()`: popups appear in place, indicators jump instead of
sliding, and continuous animation such as a progress ring or typing dots
stops.

<Tip>
  Continuous animation stays off unless it is the point of the part. A spinner
  is; a decorative shimmer isn't.
</Tip>

## Rules

- Ease out for things arriving, so they slow into place.
- Prefer `transform` and `opacity`. Animating width or height lays the page
  out again on every frame, so keep it to small, contained parts, such as a
  nav label growing into view.
- Keep motion under 300ms for anything a person waits on.
