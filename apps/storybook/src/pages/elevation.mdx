# Elevation

Most of fibo sits flat on the page, set off by a hairline `border-border`
rather than a shadow. A shadow means something is lifted: you can grab it, or
it floats over what's beneath. Shadows are Tailwind's own, `shadow-xs` to
`shadow-lg`.

## Layers

Two layers cover every part:

- **`z-10`** lifts something above its neighbours inside a part, such as a
  pinned table column or Floating nav's lifted lens.
- **`z-50`** is for layers over the page: popovers, menus, dialogs, sheets,
  toasts and a fixed nav. Base UI portals them to the end of the body, so
  their order on screen is the order they opened in.

A modal dims the page behind it with the `backdrop` token, and Floating nav's
glass variant uses `glass`, `glass-edge` and `glass-lens` rather than a
shadow.

## Rules

- Raise only what floats or can be grabbed. A card in a list stays flat.
- Every raised surface keeps its border. In dark mode a shadow barely shows,
  and the border is what separates the surface from the page.
- Don't stack levels: a menu over a dialog keeps `shadow-md`, its own level,
  not one higher.
