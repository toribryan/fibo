# Catalog

Every fibo part, grouped by job.

## Base components

The parts most interfaces need. They depend on nothing beyond Base UI,
`class-variance-authority` and `lucide-react`.

<Catalog tier="base-components" />

## Special components

Playful parts for one kind of moment, such as a diagram, a reading rail or a
reaction. Some use `motion`, which installs with them.

<Catalog tier="special-components" />

## Blocks

Whole screens composed from the parts above. A block installs into
`components/blocks/<name>/` as source you own and edit.

<Catalog tier="blocks" />
